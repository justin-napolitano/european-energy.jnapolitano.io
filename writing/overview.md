## What’s the Repo About?

At its core, this project focuses on providing insight into European energy metrics. I aim to build a platform that serves a web interface or data visualization tool for energy analytics. The idea is to streamline access to relevant datasets and make it easier for users to analyze energy consumption and production statistics across Europe.

## Why I Built This

The energy landscape in Europe is complex and constantly changing. With the push for greener solutions, it's crucial to have a platform that can aggregate and analyze this data effectively. My motivation stems from the belief that better understanding leads to better decisions. I want to equip developers, data scientists, and energy advocates with the tools they need to dig deep into energy analytics.

## Key Design Decisions

Designing this project has been an iterative process. Here are some of the essential decisions I made:

- **User-Centric Approach:** I want the platform to be easy to navigate. The interface will be designed with an end user’s needs in mind. Clean data visualizations and intuitive navigation pathways are paramount.

- **Data Integration Capabilities:** The goal is a seamless connection to various energy datasets and APIs. I want to allow users to fetch live data, making the platform dynamic and timely.

- **Modular Structure:** I aim to keep the codebase modular, allowing for easier updates and maintenance down the road. This approach also enables contributors to jump in and add new features without a steep learning curve.
